What's in it

Mascarpone, eggs, Biscoff cookies, sugar, ladyfingers and Biscoff spread.

Contains milk, egg, wheat and soy. Made with raw eggs. No nuts in our kitchen.

Questions

Is it gluten-free?

No. The ladyfingers and Biscoff contain wheat.

Is it vegetarian? Halal?

It's vegetarian, with no gelatin. No pork and no alcohol, but it isn't halal-certified.

How long does it keep?

Keep it in the fridge and enjoy it within 2 days.
